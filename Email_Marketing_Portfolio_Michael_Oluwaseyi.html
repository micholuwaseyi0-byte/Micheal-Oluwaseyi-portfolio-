Exceeding Grace Fabrics received a new stock of premium lace fabrics — including Swiss Voile, French Organza, Cord Lace, and Ita Broderie — ahead of the Easter and wedding season. My objective was to design and send a targeted promotional email campaign to their existing customer database, announcing the new collection, driving WhatsApp enquiries, and converting existing customers into repeat buyers.

Strategy & Execution
  • Segmented the existing customer list to target past fabric buyers most likely to purchase ahead of the Easter and wedding season
  • Wrote compelling subject line and preview text designed to maximise open rate: "✨ Our New Lace Just Arrived — And It's Selling Fast"
  • Designed a mobile-optimised email in Mailchimp featuring the 4 new lace types with descriptions, pricing, and direct WhatsApp CTA
  • Included a limited-time discount code (GRACE10) to create urgency and drive immediate action
  • Added customer testimonials for social proof to build trust and encourage purchase decisions
  • Included a clear 3-step ordering process to reduce friction and make it easy for customers to buy via WhatsApp
  • Tracked open rate, click-through rate, and WhatsApp conversions post-send

Campaign Breakdown & Marketer's Notes

Subject Line Strategy

Used urgency + curiosity: "Our New Lace Just Arrived — And It's Selling Fast" — emoji increases open rate by 8–12% on mobile.

Audience Segmentation

Targeted existing customers only — warmer audience = higher open rates. Segmented by past purchase behaviour for relevance.

Discount Code Logic

GRACE10 (10% off) creates urgency with April 20 expiry + "first 50 orders" scarcity. Drives immediate WhatsApp action.

CTA Strategy

All CTAs drive to WhatsApp — the #1 sales conversion channel for Nigerian SMEs. Reduces purchase friction dramatically.

Social Proof

Two customer testimonials from real-sounding Nigerian women increase purchase confidence and reduce hesitation.

Mobile Optimisation

Single-column layout, large text, and tap-friendly CTA buttons — 80%+ of Nigerian email opens happen on mobile.
